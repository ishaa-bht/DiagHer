# DiagHer 🩺💜

**AI-Powered Decision Support System for Gender-Aware Healthcare**

---

## 🌟 Overview

DiagHer is an innovative AI-powered decision support system designed to revolutionize healthcare by bringing gender-aware care directly into clinical practice. Developed by **CodeRed**, DiagHer addresses the critical gap in medical diagnosis and treatment that has historically overlooked gender-specific health considerations.

Our mission is to empower healthcare providers with the tools and insights needed to deliver more accurate, personalized, and equitable care for women patients.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔍 Gender-Specific Disease Analysis
- **Symptom Input**: Healthcare providers enter patient symptoms through an intuitive interface
- **AI-Powered Assessment**: Advanced algorithms analyze symptoms with gender-specific disease probability calculations
- **Evidence-Based Results**: Recommendations backed by current research and comprehensive medical data

### 💊 Medication Safety Analysis
- **Side Effect Screening**: Comprehensive analysis of prescribed medications for women-specific adverse effects
- **Risk Assessment**: Transparent evaluation of potential complications and contraindications
- **Alternative Suggestions**: Smart recommendations for safer or more suitable medication alternatives

### 🧠 Continuous Learning System
- **Outcome Tracking**: Integration of confirmed diagnoses and treatment outcomes
- **Adaptive Intelligence**: System becomes more accurate and nuanced with each use
- **Evidence Building**: Contributes to expanding the knowledge base of gender-specific healthcare

---

## 🎯 Why DiagHer?

### The Problem
Healthcare has traditionally been developed with a "one-size-fits-all" approach, often based on male-centric research and data. This has led to:
- Misdiagnosis and delayed diagnosis in women
- Inadequate consideration of gender-specific symptoms
- Medication side effects that disproportionately affect women
- Health disparities that could be prevented with better tools

### Our Solution
DiagHer bridges this gap by:
- Providing gender-aware diagnostic support
- Offering medication safety insights specific to women
- Learning from real-world clinical outcomes
- Empowering healthcare providers with evidence-based recommendations
